FAQ

Is my data safe?

Files are exchanged by email or a private link, never posted publicly. I delete working copies on request after delivery, and I sign a confidentiality note if your job needs one.

How do I pay?

For overseas clients: Upwork or Fiverr (buyer-protected, fees included in the quoted price). For local clients: GCash or bank transfer, 50% to start and 50% on delivery.

Is this a subscription or retainer?

No. Every job is a one-time payment. Nothing renews and no card is charged on a schedule.

What if it isn't what I expected?

One revision is included free. If the delivered work didn't match what we agreed in writing, it gets fixed or refunded.

When can you work? You have a day job.

Philippine time evenings and weekends, which overlaps well with Europe morning and US evening. I quote a delivery date before starting and tell you early if it's going to slip.

Do you take work that competes with your employer?

No. No telecom-sector jobs, no client data from my day job, and nothing that conflicts with my employment contract.

Can you do a job that isn't listed here?

Send the details. If it's a good fit I'll quote it; if it isn't, I'll say so instead of taking your money.
