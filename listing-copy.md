## First-message reply template (send within a few hours)

> Thanks — this looks doable. Based on your description I'd quote **$X**, delivered in **Y days**.
> Quick question so I don't guess wrong: **[one specific question about their file]**.
> If it's simpler than that, I'll tell you and quote lower.

send a generic paragraph.

## Where the first 10 clients actually come from

1. **Fiverr + Upwork** — set up today, apply to 5–10 small jobs a day for two weeks. Expect the
   first order in 1–3 weeks, not days. Low prices + fast replies is how a new profile wins.
2. **Your own network** — message 10 people you've worked with (former colleagues, small business
   owners you know). "I do small Excel/data jobs on the side — know anyone drowning in
   spreadsheets?" This produces the first order more often than a marketplace does.
3. **Facebook groups** — small-business and admin/VA groups where someone posts "can someone fix
   my Excel file?" Answer genuinely, then offer the paid fix.
4. **Local SMEs** — accounting offices, sari-sari store chains, small manufacturers. They have
   terrible spreadsheets and pay cash.

## Pricing reality check

- First 5–10 jobs: price for **reviews**, not profit. Undercharging is fine *only* if you ask for
  a written review afterwards.
- After ~10 reviews, raise prices 30–50%. Marketplace algorithms reward completed orders + reviews.
- Never move a client off-platform to save fees on the first order — new profiles that do this get
  restricted, and you lose the review that would have grown your account.
- Time cost matters: a $35 job that takes 8 hours is $4.4/hr. Track hours from day one so you know
  which job types to stop accepting.

## Compliance notes for your situation

- Check your Globe employment contract for a **moonlighting / non-compete clause** before taking any
  client. No telecom-sector clients, no Globe data, no work during company time or on company equipment.
- Philippine freelance income is still reportable. Keep a simple record of earnings from day one.
- Marketplaces pay in USD; GCash/local bank withdrawal has fees. Factor ~2–4% out of every quote.
